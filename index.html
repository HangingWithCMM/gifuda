Everything you need

A full-featured overlay for anything you want living on your desktop. Built by someone who watches too much anime.

🎨

True Alpha Transparency

Per-pixel alpha for PNGs, APNGs, and GIFs with alpha. Clean edges, no hacks, no artifacts.

🔑

Optional Chroma Key

Toggle on software chroma keying to make any solid color transparent. Useful for images without an alpha channel.

📁

Multi-Format Support

GIF, APNG, PNG, JPEG, WebP, BMP, MP4, WebM, AVI, MOV, MKV. If it's an image or video, Gifuda can probably handle it.

🐱

Multi-Pet Dashboard

Run as many pets as you want. Switch between them from the sidebar, each with its own settings. We're not asking what you're putting on your screen.

📐

Arrange & Rotate

Snap to screen edges, nudge pixel-by-pixel, and rotate your pet to any angle from 0 to 360.

🎚️

Full Control

Scale, opacity, FPS delay, hue, brightness, contrast, flip, resize algorithm — every parameter is adjustable, with slider + numeric input.

👻

Ghost Mode

Click-through mode lets your pet exist on screen without intercepting clicks. Great for overlays. Great for pretending to work.

📍

System Tray

Lives in your system tray. Quick access to settings, pet list, arrange, add pet, or quit.

⚡

Optimized Performance

Background worker thread, lazy pixmap building, frame subsampling, and drag-aware preview rendering keeps things mostly smooth.

Preview

A clean dashboard with a sidebar for your pets, and a compact arrange window for when they need discipline.
